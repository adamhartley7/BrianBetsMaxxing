🤖 How people use AI for betting

▾

The honest, no-hype version of what AI actually does at the betting table 👇

📊 Crunch huge data, fastForm, injuries, weather, line moves — analysed in seconds. Vendors claim 75–85% winner accuracy (take with salt 🧂).
💎 Hunt "value bets"AI estimates a true probability, then flags when the bookie's odds look mispriced. That gap is the "edge."
📐 Expected Value (EV)A bet is only good if your win-chance beats the odds' implied chance. At -110 you must win 52.4% just to break even — that's the vig.
💰 Bankroll disciplineGolden rule the AIs always repeat: never stake more than 5% on one bet. Even 95% picks lose 1-in-20.
📉 Closing Line ValueThe pro tell: did you beat the final odds before kickoff? Beating the close by 2–5% is what actually profits long-term.
🛑 Reality check: AI does not flip the odds in your favour. Sportsbooks use the same (often better) AI, set the prices, and bake in their margin. It stays a near zero-sum game — the house keeps its edge. Use AI for research & discipline, not as a money printer. 💚
